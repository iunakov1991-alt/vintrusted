FRAUD PATTERNS (ABSTRACT)

ODO FRAUD:
- Unrealistic mileage drop between records.
- Long time gap with small mileage delta.
- Fresh cluster swap with worn interior.

VIN FRAUD:
- Cloned VIN (multiple vehicles with same VIN).
- VIN plate tampering, mismatched labels.
- Auction listings with different VINs for same photos.

TITLE / INSURANCE FRAUD:
- Vehicle moves across states known for weaker branding.
- Total loss → "clean title" after interstate transfer.
- Unreported accidents (visual evidence vs report).

BUYER DEFENSE:
- Cross-check VIN across multiple data sources.
- Compare vehicle condition vs report vs price.
- Assume "too cheap" = hidden risk.
