CALIFORNIA-SPECIFIC CONTEXT (ABSTRACT)

- Smog check typically required every 2 years for most gasoline vehicles.
- Strict emissions: poorly maintained or modified vehicles often fail.
- Salvage and rebuilt vehicles must pass inspections (brake, light, possibly CHP).
- Import from other states:
  - risk of title washing,
  - out-of-state flood vehicles entering CA.
- Coastal regions: higher risk of corrosion for poorly maintained vehicles.

BUYER NOTE:
- A clean California title with consistent smog history is a positive signal.
- Gaps, out-of-state transfers, or sudden salvage/rebuilt conversions = red flag.
