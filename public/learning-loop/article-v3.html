The National Motor Vehicle Title Information System (NMVTIS) is the foundational federal database for verifying vehicle history. Authorized by 49 U.S.C. § 30501, it is administered by the U.S. Department of Justice. For a 2018 Toyota Camry registered in California, an NMVTIS report provides the core, legally-mandated title history.

Primary Data Source and Authority NMVTIS aggregates data from mandatory contributors: all 50 state motor vehicle agencies (including the California DMV), insurance carriers, and salvage/junk yards. This creates a centralized record of a vehicle's jurisdictional title events. Data points include:
  • Title Brand History: Chronological record of brands applied by states (e.g., "Salvage," "Flood," "Lemon").
  • Last Reported Odometer Reading: Date and mileage from title transfers and other state filings.
  • Total Loss History: Reports from insurance companies on vehicles declared a total loss.
  • Junk/Salvage Designations: Notifications from recyclers when a vehicle is crushed or dismantled.
Authorized NMVTIS Data Providers Raw NMVTIS data is accessible to consumers only through approved third-party providers. These entities, vetted by the DOJ, query the system and format the data into consumer reports.
