Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a vehicle history report?

Simply enter the vehicle's 17-character VIN on our homepage and complete the purchase. You'll receive your comprehensive report within minutes.

What information is included in reports?

Our reports include accident history, title records, recall information, ownership history, mileage verification, and vehicle specifications. Learn more about what's included.

How accurate are the reports?

Our reports pull data from authoritative sources including DMV records, insurance companies, and NHTSA databases. We strive for maximum accuracy and regularly update our data sources.

Can I get a refund?

If you're not satisfied with your report or encounter any issues, please contact our support team. We're committed to customer satisfaction.

Do you offer bulk reports for dealers?

Yes! Contact us for information about dealer accounts and bulk pricing options.
