Subscription & Billing Policy

Effective Date: January 16, 2025

Subscription Model

VinTrusted uses a time-based subscription model that grants temporary access to vehicle history reports during defined billing periods.

You are charged for access time, not per individual report. This means you can run multiple reports (up to daily limits) during each billing period without additional per-report charges.

💳 Pricing Structure

  • Trial Activation: $3.00
  • After 10 days: $49.00 for the next 10-day period
  • Subsequent charges: $49.00 every 10 days
  • Maximum billing cycles: 3 (after which subscription auto-cancels)

Billing Timeline Example

  • Day 1: $3 trial activation → Access begins
  • Day 11: $49 charge → Access continues for 10 more days
  • Day 21: $49 charge → Access continues for 10 more days
  • Day 31: $49 final charge → Access continues for 10 more days
  • Day 41: Subscription automatically cancels (no further charges)

Usage Limits

Access to the Service includes the following limits:

  • Daily report limit: Maximum of 2 vehicle history reports per day
  • Billing aggregation: Charges are applied once per billing period, not per report
  • Daily limits apply regardless of usage: Unused reports do not roll over to the next day
⚠️ Important: Daily limits apply even if you do not use the Service. Unused reports or access time do NOT qualify for refunds.

Automatic Cancellation

After three (3) billing cycles (approximately 40 days total), your subscription will automatically cancel. No further charges will occur unless you manually resubscribe.

Manual Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time before the next billing cycle to prevent future charges. See our Refund Policy for details.

Payment Processing

All payments are securely processed by Stripe. We do not store your complete credit card information.

Failed Payments

If a payment fails (e.g., due to insufficient funds or expired card), your access to the Service may be immediately suspended. We may attempt to retry the payment. If payment cannot be processed, your subscription will be canceled.

Refunds

All charges are non-refundable once processed, except where required by law. Please review our Refund Policy for details.
