⚠️ NMVTIS Consumer Access Product Disclaimer

About NMVTIS

The National Motor Vehicle Title Information System (NMVTIS) is a federally-mandated electronic system containing information on certain automobiles titled in the United States. NMVTIS is designed to protect consumers from fraud and unsafe vehicles.

Data Sources

NMVTIS data is provided by:

  • State Motor Vehicle Titling Agencies
  • Insurance Carriers (total loss records)
  • Junk and Salvage Yards
  • Auto Recyclers

⚠️ Important Limitations

The accuracy of NMVTIS data depends entirely on the timeliness and accuracy of data provided by reporting entities. Please be aware that:

  • Not all states participate or report complete data
  • Not all insurance carriers report total loss vehicles
  • There may be delays between an event and its appearance in NMVTIS
  • Older vehicle records may not be included
  • Not all junk/salvage yards are required to report
