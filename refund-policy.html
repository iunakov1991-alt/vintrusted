Refund Policy

Last Updated: December 23, 2025

Quick Summary: We offer a satisfaction guarantee. If your report contains no data or significant errors, we'll provide a refund within 30 days of purchase.

1. Refund Eligibility

You may be eligible for a refund if:

  • The report contains no vehicle history data
  • The report is for an incorrect vehicle (wrong VIN entered by our system)
  • You were charged multiple times for the same report
  • Technical issues prevented report delivery

2. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are not available if:

  • You entered an incorrect VIN
  • The vehicle has limited history (common for new/leased vehicles)
  • You simply changed your mind after receiving the report
  • The report contains negative information about the vehicle
  • You already received and viewed the full report

3. Refund Timeframe

  • Request Period: Within 30 days of purchase
  • Processing Time: 5-10 business days
  • Refund Method: Original payment method

4. Subscription Refunds

For our subscription service:

  • Initial $3 payment follows the standard refund policy
  • Subscription payments ($49 every 10 days) are non-refundable
  • You can cancel anytime to prevent future charges
  • Cancellation does not refund already-processed payments

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  1. Email us at support@vintrusted.com
  2. Include your:
    • Order ID or transaction ID
    • Email used for purchase
    • VIN number
    • Reason for refund request
  3. Our team will review within 2 business days
  4. You'll receive email confirmation of our decision

6. Partial Refunds

In some cases, we may offer partial refunds if:

  • The report contains some but limited data
  • There was a delay in report delivery
  • Minor errors were found in the report

7. Report Quality Guarantee

We guarantee that our reports:

  • Are sourced from NMVTIS-approved databases
  • Contain accurate VIN decoding
  • Include all available records at time of purchase
  • Are delivered within 10 seconds of purchase

8. Chargebacks

If you file a chargeback:

  • Please contact us first - most issues can be resolved quickly
  • Chargebacks may result in account suspension
  • We reserve the right to dispute fraudulent chargebacks

9. Contact Us

For refund requests or questions:

Email: support@vintrusted.com
Phone: 1-800-VIN-TRUST
Hours: Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM EST
