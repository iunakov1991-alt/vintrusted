About VIN Trust - Your Trusted Vehicle History Partner

VIN Trust is dedicated to providing comprehensive, accurate, and affordable vehicle history reports that help car buyers, sellers, and dealers make informed decisions with confidence.

Our Mission

At VIN Trust, we believe that transparency in vehicle history is essential for protecting consumers and building trust in the automotive marketplace. Our mission is to make comprehensive vehicle history information accessible and affordable for everyone, from first-time car buyers to experienced dealers.

We understand that buying a used vehicle can be stressful and risky. Hidden problems, accident history, title issues, and other concerns can turn what should be an exciting purchase into a costly nightmare. That's why we've built a platform that provides all the information you need in one comprehensive, easy-to-understand report.

What We Do

VIN Trust provides detailed vehicle history reports that compile information from multiple authoritative sources, including:

  • State DMV records for title and ownership history
  • Insurance company databases for accident records
  • NHTSA databases for recall information
  • Auto auction records for sales history
  • Police reports for collision data
  • Manufacturer databases for specifications and recalls

Our reports include everything you need to make an informed decision: accident history, title records, recall information, ownership history, mileage verification, and much more.

How Our Reports Work

Our vehicle history reports are generated through a sophisticated process that ensures accuracy and completeness:

  1. Data Collection: We pull information from multiple authoritative sources including DMV records, insurance databases, NHTSA, and auto auctions
  2. Data Verification: Information is cross-referenced across multiple sources to ensure accuracy
  3. Report Compilation: All data is compiled into a comprehensive, easy-to-read report
  4. Quality Review: Reports are reviewed to ensure completeness and accuracy
  5. Delivery: Reports are delivered instantly via our secure platform
